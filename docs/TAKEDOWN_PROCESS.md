# Content Takedown & Copyright Notice Process

**Platform:** CUETFreeMockTest.com  
**Designated Grievance & Copyright Contact:** `grievance@cuetfreemocktest.com`  
**Contact Page:** `/contact#takedown`  
**Effective Date:** November 2026  

---

## 1. Educational Non-Commercial Purpose & Fair Dealing

CUETFreeMockTest.com is a non-profit, free educational portal created to help students from all economic backgrounds prepare for the Common University Entrance Test for Undergraduates (CUET UG) conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA).

Exam papers, past questions, and answer keys are reproduced for study and research purposes under Section 52(1)(a) and Section 52(1)(i) of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, which explicitly permits the reproduction of questions and examinations for educational evaluation.

---

## 2. Provenance and Rights Classification

Every paper and practice question is catalogued with one of three rights statuses:

| Status | Meaning | System Policy Gate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `cleared` | Verified public domain, educational fair use verified, or licensed content. | Indexable (`index, follow`), included in `sitemap.xml`, standard educational support. |
| `unknown` | Default status pending editorial provenance verification. | **Restricted Gates Active**: Forced `noindex, nofollow`, excluded from `sitemap.xml`, zero advertising markup (`no-ads`). Fully usable by students for free practice. |
| `restricted` | Content subject to third-party rights dispute, publisher notice, or proprietary examination material. | **Strictly Quarantined**: Hidden from public library, forced `noindex`, zero ads, restricted from mock generation. |

---

## 3. Submitting a Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized representative and believe that any content hosted on CUETFreeMockTest.com infringes upon your copyright or violates terms, please submit a formal notice with the following details:

1. **Identification of the Work:** The title, subject, and exact URL(s) of the material claimed to be infringing.
2. **Proof of Authority:** Documentation or evidence establishing that you are the rights holder or authorized agent.
3. **Contact Information:** Full legal name, organization (if applicable), mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
4. **Statement of Good Faith:** A statement confirming that you believe in good faith that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
5. **Accuracy Statement:** A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in the notification is accurate.

**Send notices to:**  
- **Email:** `grievance@cuetfreemocktest.com` with Subject line: `[URGENT] Copyright Takedown Request - [URL]`  
- **Web Form:** `/contact#takedown`

---

## 4. Takedown Workflow & Response SLA

1. **Intake & Immediate Quarantine (Within 24 Hours):**
   - The designated administrator flags the paper or question as `rights_status = 'restricted'` in the Admin Panel (`/admin/papers`).
   - The paper is instantly delisted from public hubs and sitemaps.
2. **Review & Assessment (Within 48 Hours):**
   - The editorial team assesses the claim against official NTA public releases and fair-dealing provisions.
3. **Resolution:**
   - If the material is confirmed to be third-party proprietary, it is permanently removed or replaced with an open-source original question.
   - If resolved, the audit log records the resolution with timestamp and administrator signature.
